---
name: Uploader Pro scope
description: Product scope exclusions specified for the Uploader Pro rewrite.
---

History/session browsing and Google Photos backup are intentionally excluded from Uploader Pro.

**Why:** The project documentation explicitly removes these legacy features from the rewrite.

**How to apply:** Keep future Uploader Pro work focused on the archive, collections, favorites, tags, bookmarks, secure imports, and legacy data migration; do not reintroduce session history or Google Photos backup.
